Protean & Frenzy Improved 1.4

Requires Vampire: The Masquerade - Bloodlines, Unofficial Patch 12.0 and
Game Mod Loader. Start the game with Unofficial Patch active.

Protean

- You can enter Frenzy while Protean 5 is active. When one effect ends,
  the other continues. This prevents duplicate forms and repeated stat bonuses.
- Loading a save with Protean active restores the correct screen effect.
- The Protean 5 transformation scene plays in walking mode and after you close
  a menu. Taking an action interrupts it immediately. Feeding still transfers
  blood and extends the discipline timer as usual.
- The effects play on the return to human form, and loading a save made during
  a transformation lets the scene recover correctly.
- Fists and Claws stay in the right slots without duplicate entries. Claws
  stay available when Frenzy ends while Protean is still active. When Protean 5
  ends, you re-equip your previous weapon if you can still use it.
- The camera switches to third person before you transform. Your original view
  returns once you're fully human, unless you choose another view in the meantime.

Claws

- Male and female Claws have revised directional combos, a stationary four-hit
  chain, faster heavy finishers and revised recovery. Air attacks keep your
  horizontal position, and wide swings hit consistently across combo stages.
- Unarmed crouch movement works in all eight directions. Hand poses are improved,
  including during successful sneak attacks. The revised blocking stance keeps
  the familiar heavy block.
- Click attack up to 150 ms early to continue a combo. Each continuation needs
  a fresh click.
- NPCs such as the Serial Killer retain their original Claws attacks. Frenzy
  keeps its own Claws attacks alongside the revised player combos.
- Protean Claws and Tiger's Claws sneak attacks line up the attacker and victim
  and place you correctly afterward. Successful attacks have new sounds for the
  strike and withdrawal, and blood follows the wound.

Frenzy

- Frenzy uses suitable attacks for your selected supported melee weapon and
  restores normal weapon handling afterward. Claws and visual effects activate
  correctly when Frenzy takes over.
- Movement and collision work correctly during Frenzy in the Griffith Werewolf
  fight. Frenzy can occur in that encounter and in the Public Library.

Installation and updates

1. Close the game and back up the files the archive will replace.
2. Extract into the folder containing Vampire.exe and merge the folders.
3. If updating, copy any custom settings from your backup into the new
   Bin/loader/protean-frenzy-improved.ini before starting the game.

Settings

Edit Bin/loader/protean-frenzy-improved.ini. Set an option to 1 to enable it
or 0 to disable it, then restart the game. Each option is described in the file.

Uninstall

Close the game. Restore the files you backed up and remove the files added
by the archive.
